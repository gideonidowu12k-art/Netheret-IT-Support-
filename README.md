# Netheret-IT-Support
Brief 

  The software development company that you work for has secured a new contract to develop a digital solution for Netheret IT Support Solutions.  
  
  Netheret IT Support Solutions currently provides IT technical support services for small businesses including:  
  design and installation of IT infrastructure  
  help desk services (Tier 1 to Tier 3)  
  service and maintenance of existing infrastructure. 
  
   
  
  The client (Netheret IT Support Solutions) would like you to develop a digital solution that will: 
  enable staff to log and manage IT support requests  
  manage and schedule on-site support and maintenance jobs  
  use data to provide insights into the performance of systems and teams 
  The client has carried out some research to identify features that their existing staff suggested could be included in the   digital solution.  
  
  The features the existing staff identified are:  
  a knowledge base to help Tier 1 support diagnose basic issues  
  functionality to track job progression and communicate with stakeholders  
  accessibility features for users with additional needs  
  customised work schedules and job briefs for Tier 2 and Tier 3 support staff.
